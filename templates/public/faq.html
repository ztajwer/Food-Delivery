FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Find answers regarding ordering, delivery logistics, and table reservations.

How does thermal packaging maintain meal temperature?

All orders are sealed inside vacuum-insulated thermal boxes fitted with real-time temperature telemetry sensors to ensure dishes arrive at optimal serving heat.

How is estimated delivery time calculated?

We use Dijkstra route optimization combined with machine learning preparation time models that analyze live traffic and kitchen queue density.

Can I reserve dining tables online?

Yes, table reservations can be locked through our Table Reservation portal page.
